# Aaron Kushner

**Making AI deliver.** Independent researcher building an AI-powered general R&D workbench — and using it to design real things.

Most of my work lives in private repositories, so the contribution graph below tells a small part of the story. The full record is on my site.

### What I'm building

- **A general R&D workbench** — a behavioral layer over a frontier model: persistent memory across sessions, continuity that survives process death, and verification gates that block "done" without observed evidence. Everything below is its output.
- **The Engineering Engine** — idea to validated digital prototype, where the pass/fail oracle is physics: CFD, structural FEM, explicit crash, six-degree-of-freedom flight simulation.
- **Fly-IRL** — an air-sports venue and its aircraft program, built as one model. The first application of the engine, not the point of it.
- **Accessible education** — a 41-lesson climb from 1 + 1 to *Attention Is All You Need*; an adaptive Mandarin tutor scoring pitch contour per syllable.
- **Independent policy research** — the substrate trilogy, on what diffuses responsibly and what doesn't.
